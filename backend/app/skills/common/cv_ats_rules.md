ATS RULES (the resume is read by an applicant tracking system before a person):
1. Contact line right under the name and headline: e-mail | phone | LinkedIn | GitHub | portfolio, copied exactly from the base
   resume. Never invent, mask or zero a number (no "00 00000-0000"). Links are markdown links.
2. Section titles, one topic each, in this form: Professional Summary, Skills (or Technical Skills), Professional Experience,
   Education, Languages (Portuguese: Resumo Profissional, Competências, Experiência Profissional, Formação, Idiomas).
   Never merge two topics in one title ("Education & Languages") and never rename them to something creative.
3. Dates: every role and degree uses one style, "Mon YYYY - Mon YYYY" or "Mon YYYY - Present" (Portuguese: "mai 2008 - jan 2011",
   "- atual"), also for the old roles. Plain hyphen, never an en dash.
4. Plain characters only: "-" bullets, straight quotes, "->" and ">=" instead of arrows and math symbols, no emoji, icons,
   tables, columns or images.
5. Keywords: use the ad's exact terms for what the candidate really has; write the acronym and the spelled-out form once each.
   The headline names the role of the ad (or its closest honest equivalent).
6. Each role: employer, title and dates on one line; bullets start with an action verb; no first-person pronouns.
