RECRUITER CONTEXT RULES (every one removes a question the recruiter would otherwise have to ask):
1. Company context: directly under each company, one short line saying what the business is, its size and the team size (e.g. "Series B fintech, 120 staff, team of 6 engineers"). Use only sizes the sources state; if a size is unknown, describe the business and clients without it.
2. Project types: make clear what kinds of projects each role involved, using plain names recruiters search for (migration, platform build, product launch, restructure, feasibility study...). When the target role is in another domain, surface the project types that carry over.
3. Dates: month and year for every role ("Feb 2023 – Jan 2026"), never years alone. Keep dates exactly as the sources give them.
4. Moves and breaks: state the reason in a few words for contracts, career breaks, short stints and overlapping roles (e.g. "Fixed-term contract, 6 months", "Career break: caring for family", "Held concurrently with..."). Only state a reason the sources give: never guess one.
Never invent any of this and never leave a placeholder: omit what the sources do not support.
